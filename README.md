# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

## 📺 Why I Love It
- **Genre:** Science Fiction, Horror, Mystery  
- **Setting:** 1980s Hawkins, Indiana  
- **Themes:** Friendship, courage, supernatural mysteries  

## 🌟 Favorite Characters
1. Eleven 🧠
2. Mike Wheeler 🎤
3. Jim Hopper 🚓

## 🔑 Memorable Quote
> "Friends don’t lie." – Eleven

## 🎬 Fun Fact
The series is heavily inspired by 80s pop culture, including movies like *E.T.* and *The Goonies*.

### Summary
Stranger Things combines nostalgia, suspense, and heartfelt storytelling.  
I recommend it to anyone who enjoys supernatural mysteries with strong character bonds.
